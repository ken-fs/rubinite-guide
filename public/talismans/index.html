Rubinite talismans: full list, slot costs and best builds

All 29 talismans in Rubinite with their slot cost and where they come from — plus build guides for both halves of the game. Everything runs through one loop: Focus to find the weak point, build Marks, spend them on a Strike. Talismans either speed that loop up or bend it.

The loop your build has to serve

Focus pinpoints the boss's Pure Blood Crystal and generates Marks; longer, closer Focus generates them faster. A Strike consumes every Mark for a large burst — unless you run Mark Meister, which keeps all but one. Perfect Dodges (dodging exactly as an attack lands) also grant a Mark, which is why dodge-focused talismans are damage talismans in disguise.

Slot math: Ruby starts with 2 slots. Wichiwi sells more for Impure Crystals — first 2 cost 1 crystal each, the next 5 cost 3 each, the last costs 4. Max is 10 slots, so a build is always a real trade-off.

Builds

Early game (2–4 slots) — make Focus work first

  • Keen Gaze (2) — Marks build faster, which is every damage increase you can buy at this point
  • Pensive Eyes (2) — the gauge holds while you dodge; pairs with Keen Gaze once you have 4 slots
  • Skip damage talismans. With a slow Focus loop, Direct Hit and True Swordfighter multiply a number you rarely get to use.

Mid game (5–7 slots) — convert Marks into damage

  • Keen Gaze (2) + Pensive Eyes (2) + True Hunter (3) — True Hunter scales every Slash and projectile with each Mark, so it pays off exactly when your Focus loop is fast
  • Perfect Strike (2) instead of Pensive Eyes if you are dying during Strikes — the invulnerability window is much larger than the default
  • Bloodsucker (3) if you are burning through potions; the recharge is roughly three attack chains per flask

Real World (any slots) — buy extra lives

  • Mira's Shadow (3) — one free fatal-blow tank per fight
  • Undying Blood (3) — one revive per fight
  • Both together give you two mistakes. Everything else is preference, because under one-hit rules damage taken is binary.
  • Note: health-related talismans are dead weight here — potions and the healthbar are removed.

Boss-farming build (8–10 slots) — kill speed

  • Mark Meister (2) + True Hunter (3) + Direct Hit (2) — Mark Meister keeps your Marks, so True Hunter's per-Mark bonus never resets and Strikes stop consuming your stack
  • Strike Meister (6) if you have the slots — every Strike skill at once is the single largest damage jump in the game
  • Killer Instinct (3) is a strict upgrade over Direct Hit if you can reliably hit the white arc

Frequently asked

How many talisman slots can Ruby get?

Up to 10. You start with 2 and buy the rest from Wichiwi with Impure Crystals (1, 1, then 3 ×5, then 4).

Which talisman first?

Keen Gaze. Focus speed gates every other stat in the game.

Best talisman for Real fights?

Mira's Shadow and Undying Blood — the only way to survive a mistake.

Does Strike Meister stack all Strike skills?

Yes, all of them at once, for 6 slots. It drops from The Tree.
