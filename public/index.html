Rubinite boss list: all 13 bosses, order and difficulty

Rubinite has 13 unique bosses fought across 23 total encounters — 10 Illusion fights and 13 Real fights, because most bosses are fought twice. Below is every boss with its HP, phases, attack list, and a difficulty ranking measured by HP, attack count and fight mechanics.

The one structural thing to know: progression is linear except for four fights after the first Faye battle. The Gravekeeper and The Priest are open immediately as a choice; the other two unlock once you beat them.

Frequently asked

How many bosses are in Rubinite?

13 unique bosses. Because most are fought twice (Illusion and Real), 100% completion means 23 total fights.

Which boss is the hardest?

Alcina, The Witch — 520 HP, 12 attacks, and the Real version runs on one-hit rules. Roy, The Hunter is the hardest mid-game fight.

Can you fight bosses out of order?

Only at the branch after the first Faye fight: The Gravekeeper and The Priest are open immediately, and the other two unlock after you beat them.

What is the difference between Illusion and Real fights?

Illusion fights give you a healthbar, two Healing Potions and a visible boss healthbar. Real fights remove all three and make every hit fatal.
